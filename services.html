Questions

Common questions

Do I need to know exactly what I want before contacting you?

No. Tell us about your business and what you are trying to improve. We can discuss the situation and help identify a suitable next step.

Do you only develop websites?

Website development is one of our core services. We also help businesses with website maintenance, redesigns, and digital solutions tailored to their needs. For specialised software or application projects, we'll first assess the requirements and confirm what's feasible.

Can you build custom software for my business?

We can discuss your requirements, understand your workflow, and evaluate potential solutions. Once we understand the scope, we'll explain the recommended approach, estimated costs, and feasibility before proceeding.

Do you provide website maintenance after launch?

Yes. We can discuss suitable ongoing care options or provide support for specific updates and issues, depending on the agreed scope.

Can you help if I don’t know whether I need a website or an application?

Yes. We can first discuss the problem you are trying to solve and explore the simplest practical approach before deciding what to build.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the project's scope, features, and complexity. We discuss your requirements first, then provide a proposal outlining the estimated cost and what's included..
